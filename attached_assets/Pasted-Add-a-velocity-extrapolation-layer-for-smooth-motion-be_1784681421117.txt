Add a velocity-extrapolation layer for smooth motion between polls.
This reintroduces forward dead-reckoning but bounded to avoid the previous
backwards-snap and underground-sink bugs. Keep all existing guards.

DESIGN:
  - Each aircraft holds: displayPos (rendered), lastKnownPos + lastKnownTime
    (from most recent accepted poll), heading, gs, and vertical rate if
    available (baro_rate from ADS-B, ft/min).
  - EXTRAPOLATE every frame: advance displayPos forward from lastKnownPos
    along heading at gs, scaled by (now - lastKnownTime). Horizontal only from
    heading+gs; vertical from baro_rate if present, else hold altitude.
  - CORRECT on new poll: when a newer real position arrives, do NOT snap and do
    NOT accumulate. Compute the error between where extrapolation predicted the
    aircraft would be NOW and the new real position advanced to now, then ease
    displayPos toward the corrected track over ~1s. Overwrite, never .add().

CRITICAL GUARDS (keep all):
  1. Only extrapolate aircraft with FRESH data (seen_pos < ~10s). Stale
     aircraft (seen_pos high) do NOT extrapolate — they hold position and stay
     dimmed/grey. Extrapolating a silent transponder would invent fake motion,
     which is worse than a honest freeze. This is the key rule.
  2. Clamp displayPos.y >= 0 always (no underground).
  3. Cap extrapolation distance: never project more than gs * MAX_EXTRAP_SECONDS
    (e.g. 12s) ahead — if no fresh poll arrives, stop advancing rather than
    flying off into empty space.
  4. Keep the timestamp guard and in-flight guard.
  5. Ground aircraft (gs < 2kt) do not extrapolate.

Report RAW:
  a. The extrapolation + correction tick code.
  b. Confirm stale (high seen_pos) aircraft do NOT get artificial motion.
  c. Confirm no .add() accumulation and no backwards-snap path.
  d. Confirm displayPos.y clamped >= 0 and extrapolation distance-capped.
  e. Does a straight-and-level fresh aircraft now move perfectly smoothly
     between polls? It should.
