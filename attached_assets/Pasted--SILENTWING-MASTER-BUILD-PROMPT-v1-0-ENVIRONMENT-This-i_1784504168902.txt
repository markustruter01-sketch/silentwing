## WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

SilentWing is a silent digital co-controller platform for air traffic control. It fuses four data streams (surveillance, flight plans, weather, aeronautical data) into a live operational picture of **Brisbane Airport (YBBN)** and its surrounding airspace. The system runs quietly in the background and only speaks up when something irregular, unsafe, or predicted-to-conflict is detected. All decisions remain with the human controller. The system is advisory only.

For v1 there is **no live data feed**. Everything runs on a built-in **simulation engine** that generates realistic traffic. The simulation must sit behind a clean adapter interface so a real feed can be swapped in later with zero UI changes.

**There is NO login/authentication system. Do not build one. The app loads straight into the dashboard.**

## BRISBANE AIRPORT (YBBN) MODEL

Model the real layout, simplified but recognisable:

- **Two parallel runways**, oriented roughly NNE–SSW (headings 01/19):
  - **01L/19R** (legacy runway, western side, 3,560 m)
  - **01R/19L** (newer runway, eastern side, 3,300 m)
  - Runways separated by ~2 km with terminal precinct between/west of them
- **Domestic Terminal** (northern-central, west of 01R) and **International Terminal** (southern, western side)
- **Taxiway network** connecting terminals to both runways (simplified: parallel taxiways alongside each runway, 3–4 connector taxiways each, one main cross-field taxiway link)
- **Apron/gate areas** at both terminals (domestic: ~12 gates, international: ~8 gates)
- Geography context in the 3D view: Moreton Bay coastline to the north/east, Brisbane River to the south. Simple flat colour polygons only — no map tiles, no satellite imagery.
- **Airspace:** cylinder of interest ~30 nm radius around YBBN, surface to FL250. Arrivals flow predominantly straight-in to whichever runway direction is active; departures climb out on runway heading then fan out.

Store the airport geometry in a single data file `/src/data/ybbn.ts` (runways, taxiways, gates, terminal footprints as coordinate arrays). All rendering reads from this file. Do not hardcode geometry in components.

## SIMULATION ENGINE (`/src/sim/`)

Generates a continuous, realistic traffic scenario:

1. **Aircraft entities:** callsign (QFA/VOZ/JST/ANZ prefixes + numbers), type (B738, A320, B789, A332, DH8D), origin/destination, assigned runway, flight phase (enroute-arrival, approach, landing, taxi-in, at-gate, taxi-out, takeoff, departure, enroute-departure).
2. **Flight plans:** every aircraft has a full pre-computed plan: waypoint list with planned timestamps, assigned runway, assigned taxi route (ordered taxiway segments), assigned gate. Plans are the source of predictability.
3. **Movement model:** aircraft follow their plans with small random deviations (±timing jitter, speed variance). Approach speeds ~140–160 kt, taxi ~15–25 kt, climb-out ~250 kt below 10,000 ft. Smooth interpolation every frame — no teleporting.
4. **Traffic density:** configurable; default ~10–16 aircraft active at once, mix of arrivals/departures/ground movements. Include a morning-rush pattern: density multiplier peaks between simulated 06:00–09:00 local.
5. **Weather:** simple simulated wind (direction/speed, drives active runway direction) plus 0–2 slow-moving precipitation cells (polygons with intensity) that drift across the region.
6. **Scripted anomalies (for demo):** every 3–6 minutes the sim injects ONE of: (a) two aircraft with converging predicted paths, (b) taxi-route conflict at an intersection, (c) an aircraft deviating from its filed route, (d) runway occupancy overlap risk. These drive the alert system.
7. **Sim clock:** runs in real time by default, exposes current sim time. Supports querying state at ANY time `t` in a window of [now − 30 min, now + 10 min]:
   - **Past:** ring buffer of recorded actual states (30 min at 1 s resolution).
   - **Future:** projected positions computed by blending flight plan + current position/velocity (current state weighted heavily at T+0–2 min, plan weighted heavily at T+5–10 min). Return a per-aircraft `confidence` value 1.0 → 0.4 as horizon extends.

**DataFeedAdapter interface (the ONLY door between sim and UI):**
```ts
interface DataFeedAdapter {
  subscribe(cb: (snapshot: WorldSnapshot) => void): Unsubscribe;
  getSnapshotAt(t: number): WorldSnapshot;      // past or future
  getAircraftDetail(id: string): AircraftDetail;
  getActiveAlerts(): Alert[];
  acknowledgeAlert(id: string): void;
  getFeedHealth(): { status: 'live' | 'lagging' | 'stalled'; lastUpdate: number };
}
```

## MAIN DASHBOARD — TWO VIEWS

Tab switcher at top of screen + hotkey **`V`** toggles between:

### View A — 3D Airspace (default)
- Three.js scene of the 30 nm airspace centred on YBBN
- Camera performs a **slow continuous 360° orbit** around the airfield (one full rotation ≈ 90 s), always looking at the airfield centre, elevated ~30° above horizon
- Ground plane: dark land, darker water polygons (bay/river), airfield rendered from `ybbn.ts` (runways as light strips, terminals as simple extruded blocks)
- **Aircraft:** simple 3D markers (low-poly aircraft shape or oriented cone), coloured by phase (arrival = cyan, departure = orange, ground = white)
- **Altitude stems:** thin vertical line from each airborne aircraft down to its ground-track position — mandatory, altitude is unreadable in 3D without it
- **Trails:** fading breadcrumb trail of last 60 s
- **Predicted path:** thin dashed line ahead of each aircraft showing next 3 min of projected path (toggleable, key `P`)
- **Conflict highlighting:** when the conflict detector flags a pair, both aircraft + their predicted paths pulse amber (caution) or red (critical)
- **Weather cells:** translucent volumes over their polygons
- **Interactions:**
  - Single-click an aircraft → camera smoothly zooms/flies to it, side panel shows details (callsign, type, phase, altitude, ground speed, heading, assigned runway/gate, plan progress, any active alerts on it)
  - Double-click → **follow mode**: camera locks on and tracks the aircraft until Esc
  - Click-drag → manual orbit control; after 30 s of no input, camera eases back into the slow auto-rotation
  - **Esc = universal back-out**, one level at a time: detail zoom → follow → manual camera → auto-orbit

### View B — 2D Top-Down Airfield
- SVG/Canvas top-down of the airfield only (runways, taxiways, aprons, gates from `ybbn.ts`)
- Ground traffic + short final/initial climb aircraft shown with position, heading, callsign label
- Taxi routes: clicking a ground aircraft highlights its full assigned taxi route
- Same conflict highlighting rules, same click-for-detail behaviour
- Runway occupancy indicator strips (green = clear, amber = occupied, red = predicted overlap)

Both views render from the SAME snapshot data. Switching views never resets time position or selection.

## TIME SCRUB BAR (bottom of screen, both views)

- Horizontal timeline: **[−30 min … LIVE … +10 min]**
- Default pinned at LIVE. Drag left = replay recorded past. Drag right = **project the future**
- **Future mode rendering (non-negotiable):** aircraft render as translucent "ghosts", the entire viewport gets a distinct coloured border (e.g. purple), and a large watermark shows offset, e.g. **“PREDICTION T+07:30”**. It must be impossible to mistake prediction for live
- Ghost opacity scales with per-aircraft confidence (sharp near-term, faint far-term)
- Future conflicts detected in the projected window render with the same amber/red highlighting — this is how a controller "sees trouble before it exists"
- Past mode gets its own border colour (e.g. blue) + “REPLAY T−12:00” watermark
- Snap-back-to-LIVE button + key `L`
- While scrubbed, live data continues recording in the background; a small “LIVE continues →” pulse reminds the user

## COLLAPSIBLE SIDE TOOL TABS (right edge)

Vertical stack of collapsed tabs. Each expands into a panel. Tabs for v1:

1. **Alerts** — active alert list
2. **Conflict horizon** — table of predicted conflicts in next 10 min (aircraft pair, location, time-to-conflict, severity, one-line "why")
3. **Traffic forecast** — next-2-hours demand chart: arrivals/departures per 15-min bin, with the simulated morning-rush hump visible; highlights predicted hotspot windows
4. **Weather impact** — current wind, active runway direction, precipitation cells and which sectors/approaches they affect
5. **Alert log** — full timestamped history of every alert raised, with acknowledged/dismissed status. Dismissed ≠ deleted

**Alert behaviour (strict):**
- **Advisory:** tab glows softly only. No auto-expand. No sound
- **Caution:** tab glows amber AND its panel auto-expands with the warning
- **Critical:** tab glows red, panel auto-expands, single audio chime (one chime, not repeating)
- Auto-expanded alerts stay on screen until the controller explicitly dismisses (button) — they do not time out
- Every alert shows a one-line WHY (e.g. "QFA523 & VOZ711 converging, predicted loss of separation in 94 s near BN north approach"). Never a bare warning
- Dismissing writes to the alert log with timestamp

## PERSISTENT UI CHROME

- **Top bar:** SilentWing wordmark, view tabs (Airspace / Airfield), sim-time clock (24 h), **data feed health indicator**
- **Feed health (critical requirement):** green dot "LIVE" when snapshots are fresh; if the feed lags >3 s → amber "LAGGING"; >8 s → the ENTIRE viewport gets a red border + "DATA STALE — DO NOT USE" banner. A frozen picture that looks live is the most dangerous failure state; test this by adding a dev button that pauses the feed
- **Status strip (top, under bar):** active aircraft count, active alerts count, ground movements count, sector load %
- **Hotkeys:** `V` switch view, `P` toggle predicted paths, `L` snap to live, `Esc` back out, `Space` pause/resume camera auto-rotation
- **Settings panel (gear icon):** colour-blind-safe palette toggle (swap cyan/orange/red set for a deuteranopia-safe set), predicted path lookahead length (1/3/5 min), audio on/off
