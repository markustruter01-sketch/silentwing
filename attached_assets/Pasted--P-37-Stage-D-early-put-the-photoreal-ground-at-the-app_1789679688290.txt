[P-37] Stage D (early) — put the photoreal ground at the app's zero height.

GOAL
Aircraft are rendering below the visible photoreal ground. The app draws
ground level at Y=0, but the photographed tarmac sits above that. Measure
the difference and move the tiles down so the tarmac is at Y=0.

RISK CLASS
[BEHAVIOR-CHANGING].

DO NOT
- Do not change ALT_SCALE, toV3, or any aircraft position or altitude.
  The app's coordinate frame is correct and tested. The TILES move.
- Do not raise aircraft to compensate. That would make altitude more wrong.
- Do not change any detector, adapter, director.ts, or test.
- Do not create or commit any documentation or notes file.

STEPS
1. MEASURE FIRST, do not assume. Using the tile renderer, sample the
   actual height of the loaded tile surface at the airport reference
   point, in the app's own coordinate frame. Report the number in metres.
   If the library offers a raycast or a surface-height helper, use it. Do
   not hand-write geoid maths.
2. Report what you expected and what you measured. If the number is near
   zero, the theory is wrong — STOP and report, do not apply an offset.
3. Apply that measurement as a vertical offset to the tile world, so the
   tarmac at the airport reference point sits at app Y = 0. Put the offset
   in the same transform built in Stage A; do not add a second one.
4. Make the offset a named value with a comment saying what it is and how
   it was measured, so it is not a mystery number later.
5. Report whether this offset should be recomputed per airport. Sydney,
   Melbourne, Perth and Adelaide will each have their own. If it must be
   per airport, say how it should be obtained rather than hardcoding one.
6. Confirm afterwards that ground aircraft markers and their shadows sit
   ON the visible tarmac, not under it and not floating above it.

VERIFY
7. Run the tests, typecheck and build as usual.
8. Commit. Message: "Stage D: offset the tile world so photoreal ground
   sits at app zero [BEHAVIOR-CHANGING]". Do not push.

REPORT
- The commit hash.
- The measured offset in metres, and how you measured it.
- Your answer to step 2 and step 5.
- Raw output of step 7.
