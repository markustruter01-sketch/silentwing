## DETECTOR 1 — PREDICTED CONFLICT (straight-line projection)

**Concept:** unlike the separation detector (which fires when aircraft are
ALREADY too close), this looks AHEAD: project each airborne aircraft forward
along its current heading at its current ground speed in a straight line, and
find pairs whose projected paths bring them within separation minima within the
look-ahead window — before it actually happens.

**Method:**
- For each pair of airborne fresh aircraft, step forward in time from now to
  LOOKAHEAD_S (default 120 s) in fixed steps (e.g. every 10 s).
- At each step, project both aircraft straight-line: 
  `pos(t) = pos_now + velocity_vector * t` (horizontal from heading+gs;
  vertical: hold current altitude — straight-line horizontal only, do NOT
  extrapolate climb/descent in 6B).
- Compute horizontal + vertical separation at each step.
- If at ANY future step both horizontal < 3 nm AND vertical < 1000 ft, a
  predicted conflict exists. Record the TIME-TO-CONFLICT (first step where the
  breach occurs) and the closest approach.

**Severity by time-to-conflict:**
- **advisory:** breach predicted 90-120 s out
- **caution:**  breach predicted 45-90 s out
- **critical:** breach predicted < 45 s out

**Message example:**
"QFA523 & VOZ711 — predicted 2.4 nm / 800 ft in 72 s (converging)".

**Rules:**
- Only pairs whose separation is DECREASING (converging) — if they're already
  separating, no predicted-conflict alert.
- If a pair ALSO has an active current-separation alert (detector from 6A),
  the current breach takes precedence — do not double-alert the same pair with
  both a separation AND a predicted_conflict alert. Current breach wins.
- Straight-line only. State clearly this ignores turns (aircraft may be
  vectored) — this is a heads-up, not a guarantee.

## DETECTOR 2 — RUNWAY OCCUPANCY

**Concept:** flag when more than one aircraft is on or dangerously close to the
same runway — the classic runway-incursion risk.

**Method:**
- Use the REAL runway geometry (REAL_RUNWAYS from ybbn_real.ts). For each
  runway, build a capture corridor: within RUNWAY_WIDTH_M (default 60 m) of the
  runway centreline polyline, along its length, PLUS a short approach/departure
  extension at each end (default 500 m).
- For each aircraft, determine if it is within a runway's corridor AND at low
  altitude (alt < 500 ft OR on ground) — i.e. actually using/crossing the
  runway, not cruising over it at altitude.
- If 2+ aircraft occupy the SAME runway corridor simultaneously → runway
  occupancy alert.

**Severity:**
- **caution:** 2 aircraft in the same corridor, at least one moving.
- **critical:** 2+ aircraft in the same corridor AND both moving toward each
  other or one on the runway while another is on short final (both in the
  corridor, converging).
- (No advisory tier for this one — runway conflicts are inherently serious.)

**Message example:**
"Runway 01L/19R — MAS302 and QFA110 both in runway corridor".

**Rules:**
- Include ground aircraft here (unlike the airborne-only separation detector) —
  runway occupancy is fundamentally a ground/near-ground concern.
- State the runway ref in the message.
- One alert per runway (not per pair) — if 3 aircraft occupy one runway, it's
  one alert listing all involved.

## DETECTOR 3 — STALE-TRACK ESCALATION

**Concept:** you already dim tracks whose position data is stale (grey). This
detector ESCALATES a stale track to an actual alert when it becomes dangerous:
a track whose data is very old is a "ghost" — the controller may be looking at
a position the aircraft has long left.

**Method:**
- For each aircraft with seen_pos > STALE_ALERT_S (default 30 s), raise a
  stale_track alert.
- Severity by staleness:
  - **advisory:** seen_pos 30-60 s (track going stale)
  - **caution:**  seen_pos > 60 s (track significantly stale — position
    unreliable)
- (No critical tier — staleness itself isn't an imminent collision, it's a
  data-integrity warning. But if a stale track is ALSO near other traffic, note
  that in the message.)

**Message example:**
"KJR — track stale 47 s, position may be unreliable".

**Rules:**
- This is the ONE detector that operates ON stale tracks (all others skip them).
- Clear the alert when fresh data resumes (seen_pos drops back below threshold).
- Do not spam — one alert per aircraft, updated as staleness grows.
