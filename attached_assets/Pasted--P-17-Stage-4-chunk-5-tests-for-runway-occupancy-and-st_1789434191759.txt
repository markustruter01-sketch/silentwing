STEPS — runway occupancy
1. Read the Polyline type in data/ybbn_real.ts. Build a synthetic profile
   with ONE straight runway running east-west, 3000 m long, centred on the
   origin. Use terminalRadiusNm 30.
2. IMPORTANT: the detector flips the Y sign on runway geometry before
   comparing, because geometry uses +y = north and aircraft use +y = south.
   Write one test first that puts an aircraft plainly on the centreline and
   confirms it is detected. Get that working before anything else — if the
   sign is wrong every later test passes for the wrong reason.
3. Then test:
   - one aircraft alone in the corridor → no finding (needs two)
   - two in the corridor → a finding
   - corridor half-width: one aircraft 59 m off centreline and one at 61 m.
     Run them, record which is inside.
   - the 500 m end extension: one aircraft just past the runway end but
     within 500 m, and one beyond it
   - altitude: one at 499 ft and one at 500 ft
   - staleness: one at 60 s and one at 61 s
   - severity: two aircraft both moving above 5 knots → critical; two
     present but only one moving → caution

STEPS — stale tracks
4. Test:
   - seenPos 29 → no finding; 30 → advisory; 59 → advisory; 60 → caution
   - seenPos undefined → no finding
   - the anonymous-ground suppression: an aircraft whose callsign equals
     its id AND is onGround → no finding
   - same anonymous aircraft but airborne and moving → a finding
   - a NAMED aircraft on the ground and stale → confirm whether it is
     suppressed or reported. Run it and record the actual answer.
   - the nearby-traffic note: a stale aircraft with a fresh aircraft within
     10 nm mentions nearby traffic in its message; with none nearby, it
     does not
