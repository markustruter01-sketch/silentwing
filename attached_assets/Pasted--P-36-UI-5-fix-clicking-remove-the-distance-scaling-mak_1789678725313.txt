[P-36] UI-5 — fix clicking, remove the distance scaling, make aircraft visible honestly.

GOAL
Aircraft are unclickable, distant markers are wrongly enlarged, and
aircraft are still hard to find over photoreal ground.

RISK CLASS
[BEHAVIOR-CHANGING].

DO NOT
- Do not change ALT_SCALE or toV3. Aircraft altitude is already known to
  be compressed. Raising aircraft to make them visible would make the
  height MORE wrong, which the project charter forbids.
- Do not change aircraft positions, headings, detectors or tests.
- Do not create or commit any documentation or notes file.

FIXES

1. CLICKING IS BROKEN. Aircraft can no longer be selected. Find what the
   raycast tests against, and report whether the distance scaling added
   in P-35 changed the mesh the ray hits. Fix it so clicking works at any
   zoom. This is the priority — report exactly what the cause was.

2. REMOVE THE DISTANCE COMPENSATION added in P-35. Distant aircraft are
   now drawn larger than near ones, which looks broken and misrepresents
   the space. Restore fixed world-space sizing. Report what the scale
   values were and what you restored them to.

3. MAKE AIRCRAFT VISIBLE WITHOUT MOVING THEM. Add, for every airborne
   aircraft:
   - a thin vertical stem from the aircraft straight down to the ground
     plane, in the aircraft's phase colour at low opacity
   - a small flat ellipse where that stem meets the ground, as a shadow
   The stem gives the eye something to follow and shows the aircraft's
   position over the ground. Report whether a stem already exists in the
   codebase before adding a second one.

4. Keep the dark silhouette from P-35 but report whether it is actually
   helping. If it is making aircraft muddier against bright ground, say
   so and reduce it.

5. Ground aircraft do not need a stem. Give them the shadow only.

VERIFY

7. Commit. Message: "UI-5: restore aircraft selection, remove distance
   scaling, add position stems [BEHAVIOR-CHANGING]". Do not push.
