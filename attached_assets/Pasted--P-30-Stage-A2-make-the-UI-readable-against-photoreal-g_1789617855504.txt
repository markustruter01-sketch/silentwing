[P-30] Stage A2 — make the UI readable against photoreal ground.

GOAL
The app's panels and floating labels were designed for a dark empty
background. The background is now a photograph. Make everything readable
again without redesigning the interface.

RISK CLASS
[STRUCTURAL-ONLY] — visual only. No logic, no data, no positions.

DONE WHEN
Every panel and every floating label is legible over photoreal ground,
and the app looks unchanged when photoreal ground is off.

SCOPE
- panel and label styling only

DO NOT
- Do not change any aircraft position, altitude, scale or placement.
- Do not change toV3, ALT_SCALE, tileLayer.ts, director.ts, any detector,
  any adapter, or any test.
- Do not move any panel. Position is already settled.
- Do not restyle the app when photoreal ground is OFF. It must look
  exactly as it does today.
- Do not invent a new colour palette. Keep the existing colours and add
  backing behind them.
- Do not load tiles during your own verification beyond what step 6 needs.
- Do not create or commit any documentation, notes or summary file.

STEPS
1. Read how the existing panels are styled — the METAR stack, the
   sequence ladder, the alert panels, the keyboard help. Report what
   background they currently use.
2. Give the HTML panels a proper backing: a dark translucent background
   with a blur behind it, and a subtle border. Enough that white text on
   it is legible over bright imagery. Keep the existing text colours.
3. The floating 3D labels are the harder half — aircraft callsign labels,
   TERMINAL, runway numbers, alert boxes. These are canvas textures in
   the 3D scene, not HTML. Read how they are drawn, then give each a dark
   semi-opaque rounded panel behind the text, drawn into the same canvas.
   Report which functions you changed.
4. The alert boxes are far too large. In one screenshot a single advisory
   fills a quarter of the screen. Find what sets their scale and reduce it
   so an alert box reads as a label, not a billboard. Report the before
   and after values.
5. Apply the same to the advisory and critical alert labels so all
   floating text has consistent backing.
6. Verify with photoreal ground ON at Sydney, and take a screenshot. One
   tile request is acceptable for this.
7. Verify with photoreal ground OFF that the app looks as it did before.
   Screenshot that too.
8. Run the tests, typecheck and build as usual.
9. Commit. Message: "Stage A2: legible panels and labels over photoreal
   ground [STRUCTURAL-ONLY]". Do not push.

REPORT
- The commit hash.
- Both screenshots.
- Which label-drawing functions you changed.
- The alert box scale before and after.
- Raw output of step 8.
- Tile requests used.
