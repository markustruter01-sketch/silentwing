[P-35] UI-4 — make the aircraft visible against photoreal ground.

GOAL
Aircraft markers were designed for a dark empty background and are nearly
invisible over photoreal imagery. This is the most important remaining
defect: an air traffic display must show the traffic.

RISK CLASS
[BEHAVIOR-CHANGING] — aircraft rendering.

DO NOT
- Do not change aircraft positions, altitudes, headings or any detector.
- Do not change the colour MEANING. Approach stays blue, taxi stays amber,
  at-gate stays green, departure stays purple. Only visibility changes.
- Do not change any test.
- Do not create or commit any documentation or notes file.

STEPS
1. Report how aircraft markers are currently drawn — shape, colour source,
   size, and whether size changes with camera distance.
2. Make each aircraft readable against bright imagery. In order of
   preference:
   - give every aircraft marker a dark outline or halo so its shape
     separates from any background
   - raise the marker's brightness and saturation so it reads as a lit
     object rather than a dark one
   - add a small contact shadow or ground tick beneath airborne aircraft
     so the eye finds it
   Keep the existing phase colours. Report what you did.
3. Aircraft markers must stay a usable size when the camera is far away.
   Report whether they currently shrink with distance and what you changed.
4. The selected and alerted aircraft must stand out clearly from the rest.
   Report how.
5. Restore the full METAR and forecast: confirm the chevron on the weather
   strip opens them, and that the sequence ladder opens from the command
   bar. Report which button opens which panel. If either is unreachable,
   fix it.
6. Verify by screenshot with photoreal ground ON that individual aircraft
   are findable without reading the labels.

VERIFY
7. Run the tests, typecheck and build as usual.
8. Commit. Message: "UI-4: aircraft visibility over photoreal ground
   [BEHAVIOR-CHANGING]". Do not push.

REPORT
- The commit hash.
- Raw output of step 7.
- What you changed for visibility, specifically.
- Your answer to step 5.
