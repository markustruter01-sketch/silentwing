2. Add a dropdown with these five airports and their reference points:
   - YBBN Brisbane   -27.3842, 153.1175
   - YSSY Sydney     -33.9461, 151.1772
   - YMML Melbourne  -37.6690, 144.8410
   - YPPH Perth      -31.9403, 115.9669
   - YPAD Adelaide   -34.9450, 138.5310
   These are approximate airport reference points, good to within a few
   hundred metres. That is fine for a coverage check.

5. Show, on screen, the number of tiles currently visible. That is the
   quickest signal of whether an airport has real 3D coverage or is
   falling back to flat imagery.

   Do not push.
